⛏️ XMR Idle Miner Settings

Configure your mining wallet, pool, and performance settings.

Wallet

Your Monero wallet address (starts with 4, ~95 characters)

Mining Pool

WebSocket URL of the mining pool or proxy

Performance
4

Number of Web Worker threads for mining. More = higher hashrate but more CPU usage.

60s

How long the PC must be idle before mining starts.

How it works: Mining starts automatically when your PC is idle for the configured timeout. It stops immediately when you move your mouse or press a key. All mined XMR goes directly to your wallet via the pool.
